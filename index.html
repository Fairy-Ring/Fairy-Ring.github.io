Skills, activities and interfaces

The source census contains 20 minigame directories and 17 in-era skill directories. That is an inventory, not functional acceptance. Farming coverage remains partial, Slayer has quest-dependent gaps, and inherited multiplayer activities still need validation.

Pest Control, Barrows, the Fight activities, Champions’ Challenge and other residual activities need complete gameplay loops and rewards. Opening an interface or displaying a counter does not prove its underlying activity. World interactions and interface ownership also remain part of the completion work.
